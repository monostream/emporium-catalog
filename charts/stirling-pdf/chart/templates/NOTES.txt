Access is controlled by the Emporium login. This is the open-source (MIT) ultra-lite
build: it has no accounts or single sign-on of its own, and it has no Office
conversion, OCR or Calibre tools. Files are processed in memory and in temporary
space and are gone when the pod restarts.

The REST API under /api/v1 is behind the same Emporium login, so scripts need an
Emporium session.
