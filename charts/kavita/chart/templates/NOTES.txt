Library files go to the "library" volume, mounted at /data in the pod. The "Books" library reads /data/books (put files in sub-folders, e.g. /data/books/<author>/<book>.epub); add comics or manga as libraries on /data/comics or /data/manga.
Reader apps use OPDS: https://{{ .Values.ingress.host }}/api/opds/<your API key> (Kavita > User settings > Account). The OPDS and KOReader sync paths skip the Emporium login.
