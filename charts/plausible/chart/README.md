# Plausible

Simple, lightweight and privacy-friendly web analytics. Plausible Community Edition counts page views,
referrers and goals without cookies and without collecting personal data, so most sites need no
cookie banner.

## Features

- Lightweight tracking script (under 1 KB)
- Real-time dashboard with sources, pages, locations, devices and goals
- Shared and embeddable dashboards, CSV export and import
- Email reports and traffic spike notifications
- Team accounts with two-factor authentication

## Perfect for

- Websites and blogs that want analytics without tracking their visitors
- Teams replacing Google Analytics with a self-hosted, GDPR-friendly tool
